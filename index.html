Questions, answered straight

From Elite's own playbook — the answers Simon gives every customer, now working for him 24/7.

Why not just use a regular car wash?

Automatic brushes and harsh chemicals are exactly what put swirls and fine scratches into clear coat — then strip whatever protection was there. Elite treats paint as a craft: specialised tools, gentle methodical technique, and chemistry matched to your paint, clear coat and interior surfaces. A wash maintains a car. Correction restores it.

Why does the 5-year ceramic warranty matter?

Because a coating is a real investment, and a warranty is the maker standing behind it in writing: the gloss, water behaviour and protection have to keep performing for years — not weekends. A multi-year warranty is proof the application was done properly, with the right prep underneath it.

Mobile detailing — does the quality drop?

No. The mobile unit carries the same professional equipment, products and process to your home or office — the full Elite standard, without you losing half a day to drop-off and pickup. Your day stays uninterrupted; your car still gets undivided attention.

How does pricing work — and is it honest?

Paint has to be read before it can be priced: condition, defect severity and your goal decide the correction level. Get your instant ballpark above, Simon confirms the exact price at inspection, and price-matching is available on verified estimates. No surprises, no pressure.
